# 🏡 Airbnb Performance Dashboard | Power BI

An interactive **Power BI dashboard** built to analyze Airbnb's global performance across multiple cities. This project provides valuable insights into listings, hosts, pricing, reviews, ratings, seasonality, and market share, helping uncover business trends and customer behavior.

---

## 📌 Project Overview

This dashboard transforms raw Airbnb data into meaningful business insights through interactive visualizations and KPIs. It enables users to explore listing growth, compare cities, analyze pricing, evaluate customer ratings, and understand seasonal booking trends.

---

## 📊 Dashboard KPIs

- 📍 Total Listings: **279,712**
- 🌍 Cities Analyzed: **10**
- 👤 Total Hosts: **182,024**
- 🏠 Property Types: **144**
- ⭐ Total Reviews: **5.37M+**

---

## 📈 Dashboard Features

- Executive KPI Cards
- Listing Growth Analysis (2008–2020)
- Market Share by City
- Average Price by Property Type
- City-wise Rating Analysis
- Monthly Review Seasonality
- Host Trust & Verification Analysis
- Interactive Filters & Drill-through

---

## 💡 Key Business Insights

- Paris has the highest number of Airbnb listings and reviews.
- Paris, New York, and Sydney contribute nearly half of all listings.
- Airbnb experienced rapid growth until 2015 before slowing due to regulatory changes and the COVID-19 pandemic.
- Hotel Rooms have the highest average listing price.
- Mexico City and Rio de Janeiro received the highest overall ratings.
- More than two-thirds of Airbnb hosts are fully verified, improving customer trust.
